How to Play

• Summon heroes at the Gate with gems (earned from missions).
• Deploy up to 3 wardens on operations. They fight on their own — power, morale, and bonds decide the outcome.
• Free will: heroes train, nap, argue, and bond by themselves. Morale below 30 and they may refuse deployment. Greedy ones demand bonuses.
• Rest a hero to restore morale (30s, unavailable for ops).
• Bonds: heroes who get along grant +8% power when deployed together.
• Duplicates from the gate feed XP into the existing hero.
• Loot: missions drop equipment (⚔️ weapon, 💠 charm) — tap a hero to equip. Bosses always drop high-rarity loot.
• Skills: in battle, heroes use class skills — Oracle heals, Arcanist blasts all foes, Bulwark shields the team, Ranger snipes, Blade combos.
• Bosses appear from Threat T4+ (👹 tag): ~30% tougher, double-plus rewards.
• Lobby: idle wardens wander the courtyard. Tap buildings to build/upgrade — Training (idle XP), Tavern (morale + bonds), Moon Garden (morale), Forge (loot chance). Costs gold from missions.
• Vault & Workshop: all loot is stored here (cap grows per level; overflow is auto-salvaged for gold). Upgrade items up to the vault's level — each upgrade adds permanent PWR.
• Work: assign non-combat wardens to the Farmstead 🌾, Lumber Camp 🪵, or Quarry ⛏️ — they gather every 8s (Calm workers gather double, Lazy ones nap). Rations scale with threat tier; building requires 🪵 wood and 🪨 stone.
• Raids: from Threat T5+, the Hollow periodically attacks the citadel (🚨). Answer within 90s or a building is damaged — or pay 200 gold tribute to skip.
• Ascension: wardens at Lv10+ can ascend for gold — reset to Lv1 with +15% permanent power per ✦. Dismiss unwanted wardens for salvage gold.
• Captain: appoint a Lv10+ Loyal, Calm, or Strategist warden (👑 from their page — eligible traits show a 👑 on their card). They rest low-morale heroes, staff work stations, and auto-deploy ops and raids by your standing orders — their trait bends the rules.
• Strategist: a rare mind. Offers counsel (🧠 button in the Lobby, or speaks up on their own), talks the Captain out of reckless fights and bad raid odds, and gives unbiased odds-perfect Suggest advice.
• Telegraphs: red zones in battle show incoming strikes — high-morale wardens dodge out, low morale may freeze or panic.
• Suggest: in any deploy screen, 💡 Suggest asks a leader (your first pick, else the Captain, else the strongest) to propose a party with reasons — personality-biased advice, never a command.
• Progress saves automatically, even offline. Refresh anytime.
